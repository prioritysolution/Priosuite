PRIOSUITE / PRIO_BANK - COMPLETE PROJECT ANALYSIS
================================================================================

1. WHAT THIS PROJECT DOES (OVERALL PURPOSE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PrioSuite (or prio_bank) is a comprehensive, web-based Cooperative Banking Management System. It is designed for cooperative banks and credit societies to manage their entire day-to-day operations. The application is a Next.js (React) frontend that communicates with a remote backend REST API. 

The software digitizes all core banking workflows, including:
- Member Management (shares, groups, dividends)
- Core Banking Operations (savings/deposits, loan processing, investments)
- General Accounting (vouchers, daybook, trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet)
- Administration & Setup (system users, geographic tracking, product configurations)
- Reporting & Printing (passbook generation, pdf reports, ledgers)

The system is designed with a strict 3-tier frontend architecture:
1. Routing (app/): Simple shell pages.
2. Logic (container/): Business logic, API calls, form validation.
3. UI (components/): Visual layout and user interactions.

2. DETAILED EXPLANATION OF EVERY MODULE / PAGE WITH ROUTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The application is divided into several main domains. Below is the detailed function of every page within the system, alongside its URL route (relative to the dashboard):

A. MEMBERSHIP (Managing the cooperative's members and shares)
- issueMembership (Route: /membership/issueMembership): Enrolling a new individual member into the cooperative.
- groupProfile (Route: /membership/groupProfile): Creating and managing profiles for groups (like SHGs).
- institutionProfile (Route: /membership/institutionProfile): Creating and managing profiles for institutional members.
- mapgroupmember (Route: /membership/mapgroupmember): Linking individual members to their respective groups.
- mapinstitutemember (Route: /membership/mapinstitutemember): Linking individual members to institutions.
- memberProfile (Route: /membership/memberProfile): Viewing and editing detailed information of an individual member.
- memberEnquiry (Route: /membership/memberEnquiry): Searching and looking up member details quickly.
- shareIssue (Route: /membership/shareIssue): Issuing new shares to members.
- shareRefund (Route: /membership/shareRefund): Refunding shares if a member withdraws.
- calculateDividend (Route: /membership/calculateDividend): Computing the yearly dividend payouts for members based on their shareholdings.
- generateCertificate (Route: /membership/generateCertificate): Generating official printable share/membership certificates.
- membershipWithdrawn (Route: /membership/membershipWithdrawn): Processing the exit of a member from the cooperative.
- passbookPrint (Route: /membership/passbookPrint): Generating formatted views to print the member's passbook.
- report (Route: /membership/report): Generates PDFs for member registers, dividend lists, and transaction histories.

B. LOAN (Managing the lifecycle of loans given out by the bank)
- newApplication (Route: /loan/newApplication): Initiating a new loan request for a member.
- disburse (Route: /loan/disburse): Approving and disbursing the loan funds to the member's account.
- repayment (Route: /loan/repayment): Logging installments and collections paid by the borrower.
- generateSchedule (Route: /loan/generateSchedule): Calculating and viewing the EMI / Repayment schedule.
- guarantorDetails (Route: /loan/guarantorDetails): Adding guarantors to a loan.
- guarantorRelease (Route: /loan/guarantorRelease): Releasing guarantors once the loan is paid off.
- defaulterList (Route: /loan/defaulterList): Tracking members who have missed their scheduled loan repayments.
- accountStatement (Route: /loan/accountStatement): Viewing the full ledger of a specific loan account.
- passbookPrint (Route: /loan/passbookPrint): Printing the loan passbook for the customer.
- report (Route: /loan/report): Summaries of all disbursements and repayments.

C. INVESTMENT (Managing the bank's investments / fixed deposits)
- investmentOpenAccount (Route: /investment/investmentOpenAccount): Opening a new fixed deposit, recurring deposit, or other investment for a customer.
- investmentRenewal (Route: /investment/investmentRenewal): Renewing a matured investment for a new term.
- investmentClose (Route: /investment/investmentClose): Closing an investment account and paying out the principal + interest.
- report (Route: /investment/report): Viewing active investments and maturities.

D. VOUCHER & ACCOUNTING (Day-to-day bookkeeping)
- voucherEntry (Route: /voucher/voucherEntry): The core screen for entering general receipts, payments, and journal vouchers.
- adjustmentVoucher (Route: /voucher/adjustmentVoucher): Special vouchers to correct accounting mistakes.
- provision (Route: /voucher/provision): Setting aside funds for provisions (e.g., NPA provisions).
- daybook (Route: /report/daybook): The daily ledger showing all transactions in the bank for the day.
- cashbook (Route: /report/cashbook): Tracking all physical cash coming in and going out.
- accountLedger (Route: /report/accountLedger): Viewing the transaction history of specific accounting heads.
- subLedger (Route: /report/subLedger): Viewing transaction history for sub-ledgers.
- trailBalance (Route: /report/trailBalance): Generating the Trial Balance to ensure debits equal credits.
- profitLoss (Route: /report/profitLoss): Generating the bank's income statements.
- plAppropiation (Route: /report/plAppropiation): Profit and Loss appropriation account.
- balanceSheet (Route: /report/balanceSheet): Generating the final Balance Sheet of the bank's assets and liabilities.
- balancing (Route: /report/balancing): Tallying end-of-day balances.
- userScroll (Route: /report/userScroll): Tallying user-specific transaction scrolls.

E. MASTER (Admin configurations and setups)
- depositProduct (Route: /master/depositProduct): Defining the rules, interest rates, and terms for different types of bank accounts.
- loanProduct (Route: /master/loanProduct): Defining the rules and terms for loan products.
- depositInterestSetup (Route: /master/depositInterestSetup): Updating the interest rate structures over time.
- shareProduct (Route: /master/shareProduct): Defining the face value and limits of cooperative shares.
- depositAgent (Route: /master/depositAgent): Managing collection agents.
- operationalArea (Route: /master/operationalArea/...): Setting up the geographical areas (State, District, Block, Village, etc.) where the bank operates.
- subLedger (Route: /master/subLedger): Creating the accounting tree (assets, liabilities, incomes, expenses).
- demandMaster (Route: /master/demandMaster): Setting up rules for automated loan demands.
- passbookSettings (Route: /master/passbookSettings): Aligning the print coordinates for physical passbook printing.

F. OPENING (Migration and Year-Start entries)
- bankOpening (Route: /opening/bankOpening): Setting the opening balance for the bank's cash/bank accounts.
- ledgerBalance (Route: /opening/ledgerBalance): Setting the start-of-year balances for accounting ledgers.
- denomination (Route: /opening/denomination): Setting the denomination for opening balances.
- depositAccountOpening (Route: /opening/depositAccountOpening): Migrating opening balances for deposits.
- loanOpening (Route: /opening/loanOpening): Migrating opening balances for loans.
- membershipOpening (Route: /opening/membershipOpening): Migrating opening balances for members.
- investmentOpening (Route: /opening/investmentOpening): Migrating opening balances for investments.

G. TOOLS & RECTIFY (System Admin)
- createUser (Route: /tools/createUser): Adding new bank employees to the system.
- userRole (Route: /tools/userRole): Assigning permissions (cashier, manager, admin) to users.
- loginPasswordPolicy (Route: /tools/loginPasswordPolicy): Enforcing security rules for passwords.
- rectify/deposit (Route: /rectify/deposit): Administrative screens to fix severe data-entry errors in core accounts.
- rectify/membership (Route: /rectify/membership): Administrative screens to fix membership data-entry errors.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW IT ALL WORKS TOGETHER (THE WORKFLOW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SETUP: Admins first use the `master` module to set up geographies, products, and ledgers.
2. MIGRATION: Existing balances are entered via the `opening` modules.
3. DAILY OPERATIONS: 
   - Cashiers use `membership` to enroll people.
   - Customers open accounts via `investment` or take out credit via `loan`.
   - Money movement is logged manually via `voucher` or automatically via the modules.
4. END OF DAY/YEAR: Managers use the `report` modules to print Daybooks, Trial Balances, and Balance Sheets. 

Through standard React-Hook-Forms and Redux, data flows seamlessly from the user input -> validated through Yup -> sent via Axios to the backend API -> and recorded in the database.
